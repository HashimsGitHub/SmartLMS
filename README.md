## A simpler way to deliver learning

SmartLMS is a modern, mobile-first learning platform for organizations that want to publish video courses, connect learners with training, and manage access from one place. Its focused, role-based experience gives administrators, instructors, and learners the tools they need without getting in the way of learning.

## Features

- **Purpose-built workspaces:** Separate experiences for administrators, instructors, and learners, each with tools suited to their role.
- **Straightforward course creation:** Create and update courses with a title, description, and video lesson.
- **Flexible enrollment:** Let learners explore the course catalog and enroll themselves, or have instructors and administrators manage enrollments.
- **Clear course oversight:** Search and manage courses, user roles, and enrollment rosters from dedicated dashboards.
- **Video-based learning:** Learners can open enrolled courses, watch lessons in the built-in video player, and share lesson links.
- **Learning on the go:** Responsive layouts and installable Progressive Web App support make SmartLMS convenient across desktop and mobile devices.
- **Comfortable viewing:** Light and dark themes adapt to learner preferences, with an offline fallback experience.

## Designed for organizations

SmartLMS provides a practical foundation for delivering internal training, onboarding, and other video-based learning programs. Its learner, instructor, and administrator workflows bring course delivery and enrollment management together in a clear, approachable product.
